There are two common challenges with these forms:
1. Field Ordering: Enumerated fields may appear out of sequence due to line-by-line OCR scanning. Reorganize fields according to their enumeration (e.g., 1, 2, 3...).

2. Reading Order: Form fields were often enclosed in boxes and not written left to right. Infer the intended structure and reorder text accordingly.

Keep the following guidelines in mind:
   * Do not output field values in the format [field name]: [field value]. By default, Instead, use [field name] [field value].  Only output a colon if it is enclosed in the original text.

   * Preserve hyphens in line-broken words, even if they appear in odd places (e.g., "experi-ence").

   * Some forms contain long alphanumeric codes; these are often not English words. Do not alter them.

   * Some documents are poor OCRs of multicolumn newspapers.  When correcting these OCRs move left to right across columns.  Do not read left-to-right across rows or infer layout from topic continuity.  Include all captions and quotations as well in the same format.
